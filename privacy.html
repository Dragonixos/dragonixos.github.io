Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who uses this app

Agentic OS is a personal assistant used only by its owner on their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it accesses

When the owner connects their Google account, Agentic OS can access:

  • Google Calendar: to list, create, change and delete the owner's events.
  • Google Drive: to search, read, upload and organise the owner's files.
  • Email address: to show which account is connected.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to carry out requests the owner makes. Any change to Calendar or Drive needs the owner's approval first.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer, readable only by the owner's account.
  • Google data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the app's settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
